Rooznegar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rooznegar ("Rooznegar", "we", "us"), the to-do list web application available at https://rooz.mahsen81.ir, accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including information obtained from Google user data when you choose to connect your Google account.

1. Summary

  • Rooznegar works in your web browser. Your tasks and Google Calendar data are not sent to our servers. The only exception is optional background reminders (section 2.6), which store reminder titles and times so they can be delivered when the app is closed.
  • Google Calendar data is used only to show your own tasks and keep them in sync with your Google Calendar.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI/ML models.

2. Data we access

2.1 Data you enter

Task titles, notes, dates and times, duration, priority, categories, tags, links, sub-tasks, reminder settings, chosen sounds (including an audio file you upload) and app settings (theme, colors, language and calendar preferences).

2.2 Google user data (only if you connect Google)

Rooznegar uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) and requests these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — view and edit events in your calendars. We read event title, description, start/end date and time, time zone, recurrence rule, reminder settings, color and status, and we write the same fields when you create, edit, complete or delete a task. We also store a small amount of Rooznegar-specific information in the event's private extended properties (task id, category, priority, completion state, tags, link, sub-tasks, chosen sound) so the task can be restored on your other devices.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly — read the list of calendars you are subscribed to: calendar id, name, color and access role. This is used only to display each calendar as a colored category and let you choose which calendar a task is saved to.

Rooznegar does not request access to your email, contacts, files or any other Google service. We do not request your Google password; sign-in is handled by Google.

2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, analytics or usage data. Rooznegar contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

2.4 Staying signed in

So that sync keeps working without asking you to sign in every hour, Rooznegar uses Google's authorization-code flow: after you click Connect to Google, our server receives a one-time code and exchanges it for a Google refresh token. The refresh token is stored on our server encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and is used only to issue short-lived access tokens to your own device when it asks for one, using a random session id that only your device holds. Our server never reads your calendar with it. Choosing Disconnect revokes the token at Google and deletes it from our server immediately; you can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2.5 Google Drive backup and attachments (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings, Rooznegar asks for the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which only gives access to files that Rooznegar itself creates or that you pick in Rooznegar — never to your other Drive files. It is used to (a) save a daily backup file named "Rooznegar backup.json" with your tasks and settings in your own Drive, and (b) upload photos or files you attach to a task and link them to the Google Calendar event. These files stay in your Drive under your control; our server never receives them.

2.6 Background reminders (optional)

If you turn on Background reminders in Settings, your browser creates a Web Push subscription (an endpoint URL issued by Apple, Google or Mozilla) and Rooznegar sends our server, for that device only, the reminders due in the next 7 days: the task title, the reminder time and a short text such as "10 minutes before". Our server uses them only to send the reminder notification at the right time. They are stored in a private folder that is not publicly accessible, past reminders are deleted after 24 hours, a device that has not synced for 30 days is deleted entirely, and everything is deleted immediately when you turn the option off. No Google tokens, calendar ids, notes or other event details are sent to our server.

2.7 Planning assistant (optional)

If you use the ✨ planning assistant, the text you type and a short planning context — today's date, your time zone, the names of your calendars and the start/end times of your upcoming tasks (no titles, notes or other event details) — are sent through our server to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI or GapGPT) to generate a suggested plan. Nothing is added to your calendar until you confirm it. Our server does not store the request or the answer. Google user data is not used to train AI models. Each provider processes the request under its own terms.

3. How we use the data

All data is used solely to provide the features you see in the app:

  • show your tasks and calendar events in list, week, month and statistics views;
  • create, update and delete events in your Google Calendar when you change tasks in Rooznegar, and update Rooznegar when you change events in Google Calendar;
  • show reminder notifications at the times you set;
  • show each Google calendar as a category with its color.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, credit-worthiness, profiling, or for training generalized or non-personalized AI/ML models, and no human at Rooznegar reads your data.

4. Storage, retention and security

  • Your tasks, settings and the temporary Google access token are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Requests to the Google Calendar API are sent directly from your browser to Google over HTTPS (TLS). They do not pass through our servers.
  • The Google access token is short-lived (about one hour) and is kept only in your browser. The refresh token described in section 2.4 is stored encrypted on our server and is deleted when you disconnect.
  • Data remains on your device until you delete it (see section 6). Events in Google Calendar are kept by Google under your control.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your data, including Google user data, to any third party. Rooznegar communicates with Google (for sign-in and the Google Calendar API) and, only if you enable background reminders, with your device's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) to deliver the notification. Push notifications sent through these services contain no content; the device fetches the reminder text from our server over HTTPS.

6. Your choices: access, revocation and deletion

  • Disconnect Google: in Rooznegar open Settings → "Disconnect". This revokes the access token and removes Google data from the app on that device.
  • Revoke access at Google: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: clear the site data for rooz.mahsen81.ir in your browser settings. You can also export your data first (Settings → Export JSON).
  • Calendar events created by Rooznegar can be deleted in Rooznegar or directly in Google Calendar.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still contact us with any request.

7. Limited Use disclosure

Rooznegar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Children

Rooznegar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes affecting Google user data will be shown in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact

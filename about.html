Rooznegar

Rooznegar is a free to-do list app that keeps your tasks in sync with your own Google Calendar. You add tasks with a date, time, reminders and repeat rules; Rooznegar saves them as events in your Google Calendar, and events you create or change in Google Calendar appear in Rooznegar as tasks. Everything runs in your browser — no account on our side, no server storing your data.

Open Rooznegar

No sign-up is required to read this page. You can use Rooznegar without Google; connecting Google Calendar is optional and is done from Settings inside the app.

What you can do with Rooznegar

Plan tasks
Create tasks with date, start time, duration, priority, notes, sub-tasks, tags and links.
Two-way Google Calendar sync
Tasks become events in the Google Calendar you choose; changes in either place sync within seconds.
Reminders & sounds
Several reminders per task with browser notifications, snooze and selectable alarm sounds.
Recurring tasks
Daily, weekly on chosen weekdays, monthly or yearly — the same repeat rules as Google Calendar.
Categories & views
Each of your calendars is a colored category. Today, upcoming, week, month (Persian or Gregorian) and stats views.
Focus timer
A built-in Pomodoro timer for any task.

How Rooznegar uses Google data

When you choose Connect to Google, Rooznegar asks for two permissions:

  • calendar.events — to read your events (shown as tasks) and to create, update and delete events when you add, edit, complete or delete tasks in Rooznegar.
  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to read the names and colors of your calendars so each one can be shown as a category and chosen as the destination of a task.

This data is used only to show you your own tasks and keep them in sync. It is processed in your browser, never sent to our servers, never sold, shared or used for advertising. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
